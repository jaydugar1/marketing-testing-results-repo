# Lantern Design System

## Company Overview

**Lantern Specialty Care** is a healthcare navigation company that guides patients to trusted, high-performing surgical providers and specialist care. Their brand positioning is as "a guiding light to excellent care" — a company that reduces complexity in the healthcare system and helps patients make confident, informed decisions about their surgical and specialty care journey.

**Core Value Proposition:** Lantern connects patients with a curated network of excellent surgical providers, offering support through Care Advocates, clinical navigation, and benefit programs.

**Tagline/Positioning:** "A guiding light to excellent care" — warm, expert, trustworthy.

## CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS

### Tone & Voice

- **Warm, expert, and guiding** — Lantern speaks like a knowledgeable friend, not a corporate entity.
- **Empowering** — copy focuses on patient agency and confidence ("guide patients," "trusted providers," "excellent care").
- **Clear and accessible** — healthcare is complex; Lantern simplifies it. Avoid jargon. Plain language is preferred.
- **Supportive** — the brand acknowledges that navigating surgery and specialty care is stressful. Tone is never clinical or cold.

### Person & Perspective

- **Second-person ("you/your")** in patient-facing copy — direct, personal, reassuring.
- **First-person plural ("we/our")** for company voice — "our practice," "our brand," "our Care Advocates."

### Casing

- **Title Case** for headlines and section headers.
- **Sentence case** for body copy and CTAs.
- Brand name: Always **"Lantern"** — never all-caps, never lowercase.

### Emoji Usage

- Lantern has **custom illustrated emoji** (see Emoji Reference Sheet) used across patient communications (email, chat, messaging).
- Custom emoji replace standard unicode emoji for brand consistency.
- 16 characters: Bandaged, Crying, Confused, Disappointed, Dizzy, Confident, Happy, Stressed, Sleepy, Worried, Hugging, Like, Loved, Positive, Relieved, Sick.
- Emoji are used to add warmth and relatability in messaging contexts, **not** in formal documents.

### Copy Examples (from brand materials)

- *"A guiding light to excellent care"*
- *"Benefit designed to guide patients to trusted, high-performing surgical providers"*
- *"Care Advocate"* (always capitalized — it's a title)
- *"Network of Excellence"* (capitalized — proper name)

## VISUAL FOUNDATIONS

### Color Palette

| Name | Hex | RGB | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Green | `#005042` | 0, 80, 66 | Headlines, strong CTAs, high-contrast impact moments |
| Light Cream | `#FEF8E8` | 254, 248, 232 | Call-out boxes, text on dark backgrounds, backgrounds |
| Espresso | `#311E04` | 49, 30, 4 | Subheadlines and body copy |
| Pink | `#E45F95` | 228, 95, 149 | Accent color |
| Coral | `#FF6947` | 255, 105, 71 | Accent color |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Standard background color |

**Color Vibe:** Warm and earthy (Espresso, Cream) with clinical confidence (deep Green) and human warmth (Pink, Coral). Not a cold blue/grey system — this is a warm, human healthcare brand.

**Background approach:** Light backgrounds (White, Light Cream) are primary. Dark backgrounds use deep Green or Espresso. **Flat brand colors only — no gradients, no frosted glass.**

**Deck / presentation rule:** All body and content slides must use **white** backgrounds. Cream and Green variations are permitted only for **cover, section/topic, and closing** slides.

### Typography

| Role | Font | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headlines | **Poppins** | Bold weight; geometric, friendly sans-serif |
| Body Copy | **DM Sans** | Clean, legible, modern sans-serif |
| System/Office | **Aptos** | Used in PowerPoint, Word, Outlook |

> **Font substitution note:** Poppins and DM Sans are available on Google Fonts and are used in the web design system. Aptos is a Microsoft system font used in Office environments only.

### Imagery

- **Style:** Bright, friendly clinical, lifestyle, and professional imagery.
- **Color vibe:** Warm, natural, human — not sterile or overly clinical. Real people in real settings.
- **Subjects:** Patients, Care Advocates, doctors/surgeons, lifestyle moments around health and recovery.
- **Treatment:** Clean, no heavy filters. Natural color grading. Photos feel approachable and warm.
- **Sources:** Brand Resource Center (BRC) Photo Library.
- **Full-bleed usage:** Photos used as full-bleed backgrounds with text overlaid (logo must have sufficient contrast).

### Visual Style Motifs

- **Flat, warm brand colors** — Green, Cream, White, Espresso. No gradients, no frosted glass, no backdrop-filter effects in any exported design material.
- **Soft rounded geometry** — Cards and surfaces use modest rounded corners (8–24px) consistent with Poppins' friendly forms.
- **Imagery-led variety** — When variety is needed, lead with brand photography (BRC photo library) rather than gradients or texture effects.

### One-Pagers (Marketing Collateral)

See `brand/one-pager-guidelines.md` for the full system. Reference: `uploads/CP-008 Lantern_Overview.pdf`.

Key patterns:

- **White background** with optional Cream callout panels
- **Page headline** with bold brand word inside body weight (`**Lantern** is built to deliver...`)
- **Green eyebrows** with colon-tagline pattern (`Network of Excellence: Best-in-class specialists`)
- **Huge green stat numbers** with small units inline
- **Green checkmark lists** in multi-column grids
- **Circular headshot grids** for team / care team
- **Compliance footer** with copyright line
- Coral/Pink permitted only in map dots / data viz on these sheets
- **All documents to be formatted to 8.5" x 11" letter with half inch to full inch margins**

### Long-Form Documents (eBooks, Guides, White Papers)

See `brand/long-form-guidelines.md` for the full system. Reference: `uploads/CP-002 Modern Guide to Cancer Care Benefits.pdf`.

Five page-type system:

- **Cover** — full-bleed warm photo + green wave + green title below
- **Part divider** — cream top half (`PART ONE` eyebrow + green title) / full-bleed photo bottom half
- **Content** — white bg, running header `Doc Title | page #`, green H2s, Espresso body
- **Quote / callout** — cream box, big green `"` glyph, dash-prefix attribution
- **Back cover** — CTA, About Lantern blurb, `lanterncare.com`, copyright line
- **All documents to be formatted to 8.5" x 11" letter with half inch to full inch margins**

### Deckware (PowerPoint, Keynote, Pitch Decks)

See `brand/deckware-guidelines.md` for the full system. References: `uploads/Presentation1.pdf` (official PPT template) and `uploads/Employer Pitch Deck 2026.pdf` (2026 pitch deck).

Text hierarchy (from the official template):

- **Headers**: Aptos/Poppins Semibold 52pt, sentence case
- **Sub-headers**: Aptos/DM Sans Regular 28–20pt, Title Case
- **Stat numbers**: Aptos/Poppins Bold 44–28pt, use Green most often
- **Body**: Aptos/DM Sans Regular 18–12pt, sentence case, bullets without periods

Signature slide layouts:

- **Title** — Lantern logo + tagline top-left, big green headline, photo mosaic on right
- **Stat headline** — green headline + cream stat card + stacked colored spec cards (chart slides may use coral/pink)
- **Case study** — 2×2 huge green stat grid + cream sidebar with client meta and quote
- **Member journey** — horizontal timeline with green dot markers (Lantern mark inside)
- **Comparison** — Their solution + Our solution = Best of Both Worlds
- **Closing** — numbered next steps with green numerals

**No gradients** — sunset from brand. Use flat Green or flat Cream for title/closing slides.

### Logo Usage

- **Three versions:** Green (on light backgrounds), Light Cream (on dark backgrounds), Black (print only, no color).
- **Preferred:** Green logo on light background.
- **Minimum size:** 1 inch / 96px wide digital.
- **Clear space:** Equal to cap-height of logo text on all sides.
- **Co-branding:** Divider line between logos, sized for visual balance.

### Cards & UI Elements

- Cards use flat brand colors — White (with 50% Espresso border), Cream, or Brand Dark.
- Rounded corners consistent with Poppins' soft geometric style.
- Light Cream (#FEF8E8) used for callout boxes — a warm card background.

### Borders & Shadows

- No explicit border-radius values defined in brand guide. Inferred: medium rounding (\~8–12px) consistent with Poppins' friendly aesthetic.
- Shadow approach: Soft, warm-toned shadows (not cold grey) consistent with the warm color palette.

### Animation

- No specific animation guidelines in provided materials. Inferred: smooth, professional transitions consistent with a health-tech brand. Fades and gentle slides preferred over bouncy animations.

### Iconography

- Custom icon library with \~70+ icons (see Icon Reference Sheet).
- **Style:** Filled/solid icons, likely custom-illustrated to match brand warmth.
- **Categories:** Company, Communications, Actions, Business & Payment, Healthcare, Holidays & Events, Travel & Transportation.
- Icons include: Care Advocate Person, Support Ticket, Stethoscope, Doctor, Surgeon, Medicine, Hospital, etc.
